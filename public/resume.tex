\begin{rSection}{Summary}
\raggedright
I am an Applied Scientist at Adobe, and my interests lie in multimodal language models. At Adobe, I train and evaluate them for creative work. The work often hinges on subjective quality, like whether a photo edit looks better or marketing copy feels original, so I spend a lot of my time building evaluations that can be used to reliably train and compare models. I have shipped this work in Adobe GenStudio and published it at CVPR, ICML, and AAAI workshops.

\begin{rSection}{Experience}

\begin{rSubsection}{Applied Scientist {\mdseries -- Adobe Inc., San Jose, CA}}{June 2021 -- Present}{}{}

\item[] \vspace{0.3em}{\subhead{Vision-Language Model Training \& Evaluation}}
\item Led {\bf ATune} (under review): a 54M-parameter model that learns photo retouching from 10M unedited photographs and operates \mbox{off-the-shelf} editors by setting their sliders. With 157 raters, its edits beat GPT-6 Astra using the same editor ({\bf 54.3\%} of 3,524 votes, 95\% CI 50.3--58.3) and tied with Claude Opus 5.
\item Fine-tuned {\bf Gemma-3-12B} with LoRA to score \mbox{marketing-image} quality, training on 8$\times$ H100 (DDP, BF16, FlashAttention-2, Liger kernels).
\item Grounded VLM quality reasoning in 14+ \mbox{pixel-level} metrics (sharpness, exposure, color balance); the resulting interpretable scorer reaches {\bf MAE 0.77} (Pearson r $\approx$ 0.88) vs.\ 1.34 for \mbox{zero-shot} GPT-5.4 and 1.95 for Claude Opus 4.7. Also built {\bf MQCore}, an evaluation score for \mbox{marketing-image} quality.

\item[] \vspace{0.3em}{\subhead{Evaluation Research \& Benchmarking}}
\item Created {\bf DistortBench} (first author; CVPR 2026 and ICML 2026 Workshops): a 13.5K-question benchmark over 27 distortion types and 5 severity levels. Evaluated 18 VLMs with bootstrap confidence intervals; the best model (Qwen3.5-27B) scores {\bf 61.9\%} (chance 25\%), above the average annotator (60.2\%) and just below a \mbox{3-annotator} majority vote (65.7\%).
\item Co-authored {\bf JudgeBreak} (ICML 2026 Workshop): across 95 subjective settings, linear probes on Qwen3-32B hidden states with \mbox{cross-validated} layer selection reach {\bf 78.1\%} mean pairwise accuracy vs.\ 70.1\% for the best reward model or \mbox{zero-shot} GPT-5.4 judge.
\item Co-authored a curiosity-driven {\bf LLM-as-a-judge} (AAAI 2026 Workshop, Oral) that personalizes \mbox{creative-writing} evaluation by modeling the judge's surprise at each expert's explanation, raising mean F1 from 0.37 to 0.62 and Pearson r from 0.15 to 0.56 over an SFT baseline. Trained its baseline models.

\item[] \vspace{0.3em}{\subhead{Production Multimodal Systems \& Agents}}
\item Prototyped {\bf Adobe GenStudio for Performance Marketing} (launched October 2024, now serving Fortune 500 clients including Microsoft and AT\&T), then designed its multimodal brand service, now deployed in 3 regions, for VLM content validation, \mbox{brand-DNA} extraction, and retrieval.
\item Built a {\bf multimodal Photoshop agent} that edits images from \mbox{natural-language} instructions, running an \mbox{observe-think-act} loop over 49 tools with vision grounding and a \mbox{quality-critic} refinement step.
\end{rSubsection}

\begin{rSection}{Projects}

\textbf{VibeNanoChat: GPT-2 Scale LLM Pretraining from Scratch} \hfill 2026 \textbar\ \href{https://github.com/divyanshu25/VibeNanoChat}{code} \\
{\raggedright Decoder-only transformer (RoPE, RMSNorm, sliding-window attention) with one-knob depth scaling and a custom distributed {\bf Muon} optimizer (ZeRO-2 state sharding), trained in BF16 with FlashAttention-3 at over 1M tokens/sec on 8$\times$~H100. Fit isoFLOP curves across 40+ runs (44M to 522M parameters) to find \mbox{compute-optimal} model sizes.
Write-ups: \href{https://medium.com/@divyanshugoyal/going-fast-every-optimization-that-made-llm-training-fly-f465f3cf3588}{Going Fast} and \href{https://medium.com/@divyanshugoyal/a-lazy-engineers-guide-to-scaling-transformers-104e404e2f91}{A Lazy Engineer's Guide to Scaling Transformers}.\par}
