\begin{rSection}{Experience}

\begin{rSubsection}{Applied Scientist {\mdseries -- Adobe Inc., San Jose, CA}}{June 2021 -- Present}{}{}
\item[] \vspace{0.3em}{\subhead{FixIt: Multimodal Models for Marketing and Photo Quality (Tech Lead, team of 7)}}
\item Tech lead for three multimodal models: a marketing VLM, judge models, and the ATune retoucher. Defined the data annotation strategy, the training pipeline, and the evaluation protocol, keeping training stable and fast and verifying that each model learned its intended reward.
\item Demonstrated {\bf \mbox{sub-second}} decisions on marketing content. The demos secured {\bf \mbox{org-wide} investment} in these models and led to customer engagements that {\bf opened new revenue streams}.

\item[] \vspace{0.3em}{\subhead{Adobe Marketing VLM (Gemma-3-12B, SFT and GRPO)}}
\item \mbox{Fine-tuned} {\bf Gemma-3-12B} with distributed SFT followed by \mbox{multi-round} GRPO on 8$\times$~H100, using LoRA at rank 256 (1.19B trainable parameters). Each GRPO round samples a group of answers per prompt, scores them against ground truth, and updates the policy with a clipped \mbox{policy-gradient} loss.
\item Designed programmatic rewards for 7 tasks: marketing quality, distortion type and severity, emotion, audience, attention, creative attributes, and photo observables.
\item Diagnosed {\bf reward hacking}. Rollout audits showed the policy collapsing onto hedged answers: it gave the middle quality rating to 84\% of images, against 56\% for human reviewers. Traced the cause to the reward's shape and redesigned the scorers.
\item Built the data and serving stack: an asynchronous VLM annotation pipeline with chunked checkpoints and crash recovery, and a \mbox{router-and-worker} inference API across 4 GPUs.

\item[] \vspace{0.3em}{\subhead{JEV-Style Marketing Judge Models}}
\item Trained \mbox{Qwen3.5-4B} LoRA judges on a {\bf \mbox{1.1M-example} dataset} curated from 14 \mbox{human-rated} sources, using the spread of rater opinions as soft labels.
\item Raised correlation with human ratings on KonIQ to {\bf 0.663 SRCC}, up from 0.421 for the reference model, after calibrating the score output on crowd ratings (paired 95\% CI above zero).
\item Built a {\bf \mbox{67K-item} held-out benchmark}, \mbox{leak-checked} with SSCD copy detection against all 537K training images. It gated every release from v9 to v19 through a \mbox{pre-registered} \mbox{non-inferiority} test. Served the judge at 21 replicas across 8 H100s.
\item Applied the judge to pairwise crop selection, reaching {\bf 0.82 accuracy} on GAIC vs.\ 0.71 for the \mbox{score-based} baseline.
\item Detected a run whose vision encoder collapsed within 200 steps, leaving every checkpoint blind to the image, and added a \mbox{vision-health} check to the training pipeline.

\item[] \vspace{0.3em}{\subhead{ATune: Unsupervised Photo Retouching (Co-Lead with Adobe Research)}}
\item Co-led the project with Adobe Research as \mbox{co-first} author (under review). Mentored an intern and owned the synthetic data pipeline, the training recipe, and the evaluation.
\item Trained a {\bf \mbox{54M-parameter} model} that retouches photos by setting the sliders of \mbox{off-the-shelf} editors (RawTherapee and darktable). It learns from {\bf 10M unedited photographs, with no expert edits}.
\item In a human study with 157 raters and 3,524 judgments, its edits {\bf beat GPT-6 Astra} using the same editor (54.3\% of decisive votes, 95\% CI 50.3--58.3) and {\bf matched Claude Opus 5} (50.5\%). On FiveK, PPR10K, and SPAQ, at least 8 of 9 published \mbox{image-quality} judges also prefer it to GPT-6 Astra.
\item Ran a \mbox{data-scaling} study from 1M to 10M photographs: each larger corpus wins or ties, and no judge on any test set prefers a smaller one.

\item[] \vspace{0.3em}{\subhead{Adobe GenStudio for Performance Marketing (Tech Lead, team of 20+)}}
\item Took GenStudio from {\bf prototype to a \mbox{multi-million-dollar} product} used by {\bf Fortune 500 companies} (launched October 2024). Tech lead for the production API server that connects the product to cloud LLM providers at scale.
\item \mbox{Co-invented} a system that uses generative models to turn a company's source material into structured brand guidelines, generate marketing content from them, and score that content against each guideline (\href{https://patents.google.com/patent/US20250117829A1/en}{US20250117829}). Designed the \mbox{brand-compliance} evaluation; the work became the product {\bf \href{https://business.adobe.com/products/brand-intelligence.html}{Adobe Brand Intelligence}}.
\item \mbox{Fine-tuned} custom models on a Fortune 500 customer's own data, which led to a {\bf \mbox{custom-models} deal}.
\item Built a multimodal agent that edits images in Photoshop, selecting from 49 tools with vision grounding.

\item[] \vspace{0.3em}{\subhead{Evaluation Research}}
\item {\bf DistortBench} (first author; CVPR and ICML 2026 workshops): a benchmark of 13.5K questions over 27 distortion types and 5 severity levels. The best of 18 VLMs (Qwen3.5-27B, 61.9\%) beats the average annotator (60.2\%) but trails a \mbox{3-annotator} majority vote (65.7\%).
\item {\bf JudgeBreak} (ICML 2026 workshop): showed that linear probes on \mbox{Qwen3-32B} hidden states beat the best reward model or LLM judge across 95 subjective settings (78.1\% vs.\ 70.1\% pairwise accuracy).
