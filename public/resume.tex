\item Tech lead for a \mbox{7-person} team, end to end across three models: a \mbox{Gemma-3-12B} GRPO policy, \mbox{Qwen3.5-4B} judges, and the ATune retoucher. Owned the annotation strategy, training pipeline, and evaluation protocol.

\item[] \vspace{0.3em}{\subhead{Adobe Marketing VLM (Gemma-3-12B, SFT and GRPO)}}
\item Trained Gemma-3-12B (LoRA $r{=}256$, 1.19B trainable parameters) with distributed {\bf SFT}, then \mbox{multi-round} {\bf GRPO} on 8$\times$~H100: \mbox{group-sampled} completions per prompt, programmatic rewards against ground truth, clipped \mbox{policy-gradient} updates.
\item Designed rewards for 7 tasks (marketing quality, distortion type/severity, emotion, audience, attention, creative attributes, photo observables). Diagnosed {\bf reward hacking} through \mbox{mode-collapse} audits of rollouts; redesigned the scorers.
\item Built an async VLM annotation pipeline with chunked checkpointing and crash recovery, plus a \mbox{router--worker} inference API across 4 GPUs.

\item[] \vspace{0.3em}{\subhead{JEV-Style Marketing Judge Models}}
\item Built a \mbox{1.1M-example} training corpus from 14 \mbox{human-rated} sources, with \mbox{rater-disagreement} distributions as soft labels. Trained {\bf Qwen3.5-4B} LoRA judges on sampled data mixtures.
\item Calibrated the score head on crowd ratings, lifting validation SRCC on KonIQ from 0.421 (reference) to {\bf 0.663} (paired 95\% CI ${>}\,0$). Built the 67K-item held-out {\bf benchmark suite}, \mbox{leak-checked} with SSCD copy detection against all training images.
\item Shipped releases v9--v19 behind a \mbox{non-inferiority} gate on that suite; served at 21 replicas across 8 H100s. As a pairwise judge for crop selection: {\bf 0.82} accuracy on GAIC vs.\ 0.71 for the score head.
\item Detected \mbox{vision-encoder} collapse within 200 steps (\mbox{image-blind} checkpoints); added a \mbox{vision-health} guard to the training loop.

\item[] \vspace{0.3em}{\subhead{ATune: Unsupervised Photo Retouching (Co-Lead with Adobe Research)}}
\item Co-led with Adobe Research (\mbox{co-first} author, under review); mentored an intern. Owned the synthetic data pipeline, training recipe, and evaluation protocol.
\item {\bf 54M-parameter} model trained on 10M unedited photographs with no expert edits. Predicts slider settings for \mbox{off-the-shelf} editors (RawTherapee, darktable) through \mbox{per-editor} solvers.
\item Human 2AFC study (157 raters, 3,524 judgments): beats GPT-6 Astra on the same editor ({\bf 54.3\%} of decisive votes, 95\% CI 50.3--58.3); ties Claude Opus 5 (50.5\%, CI 46.8--54.2). On FiveK, PPR10K, and SPAQ, at least {\bf 8 of 9} published IQA judges prefer it to GPT-6 Astra.
\item \mbox{Data-scaling} study (1M--10M photographs): each larger corpus wins or ties; no judge prefers a smaller one on any test set.

\item[] \vspace{0.3em}{\subhead{Adobe GenStudio for Performance Marketing (Tech Lead, team of 20+)}}
\item Tech lead from inception to first prototype, then for the production API server integrating the UI with cloud LLM providers at scale. Launched October 2024; now a {\bf \mbox{multi-million-dollar}} product used by Fortune 500 companies.
\item Defined the evaluation framework for brand compliance of generated content.
\item \mbox{Fine-tuned} custom models exclusively on a Fortune 500 enterprise customer's data, leading to a GenStudio \mbox{custom-models} deal.
\item Built a multimodal {\bf Photoshop agent} with \mbox{vision-grounded} tool use.

\item[] \vspace{0.3em}{\subhead{Evaluation Research}}
