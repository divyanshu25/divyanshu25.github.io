\begin{rSection}{Experience}

\begin{rSubsection}{Applied Scientist {\mdseries -- Adobe Inc., San Jose, CA}}{June 2021 -- Present}{}{}

\item[] \vspace{0.3em}{\subhead{VLM Fine-Tuning with SFT and Reinforcement Learning (Gemma-3-12B)}}
\item Built distributed {\bf SFT} and \mbox{multi-round} {\bf GRPO} pipelines to train Gemma-3-12B (12.2B parameters; LoRA rank 256, 1.19B trainable) to assess \mbox{marketing-image} quality on 8$\times$~H100: each RL round samples a group of completions per prompt, scores them with programmatic rewards against ground truth, and trains with a clipped \mbox{policy-gradient} loss.
\item Audited GRPO runs for {\bf reward hacking} and found the policy learning safe guesses: over 20 rounds, one magnitude word grew from 55\% to a peak of 89\% of its answers, and in another run it gave the middle quality rating to 84\% of images when reviewers gave it to 56\%. Traced the causes to the reward's shape, an unlearnable label, and lost exploration, and redesigned the scorers.

\item[] \vspace{0.3em}{\subhead{Multimodal Judge Models}}
\item Built a 1.1M-row training set from 14 \mbox{human-rated} sources with rater disagreement as soft labels, and trained {\bf Qwen3.5-4B} LoRA judges on mixes sampled from it. With a score readout refit on crowd labels, the served model raised validation SRCC over the reference model from {\bf 0.421 to 0.663} on KonIQ and from 0.395 to 0.595 on KADID (paired 95\% CIs above 0).
\item Built a held-out {\bf benchmark suite}: 8 sets, 67,168 items, \mbox{leak-checked} with SSCD against all 537,282 training images, with a \mbox{pre-registered} bootstrap \mbox{non-inferiority} release gate.

\item[] \vspace{0.3em}{\subhead{Unsupervised Photo Retouching (ATune)}}
\item Led {\bf ATune} (co-first author, under review): a 54M-parameter model that learns photo retouching from 10M unedited photographs with no expert edits and operates \mbox{off-the-shelf} editors by setting their sliders. With 157 raters, its edits beat GPT-6 Astra using the same editor ({\bf 54.3\%} of decisive votes, 95\% CI 50.3--58.3; 3,524 answers) and tied with Claude Opus 5 (50.5\%, CI 46.8--54.2).
\item Evaluated ATune on FiveK, PPR10K, and SPAQ with 9 published judges and \mbox{Holm-corrected} paired tests: on every set, at least {\bf 8 of 9} prefer it to GPT-6 Astra and at least 7 of 9 to seven learned retouchers.

\item[] \vspace{0.3em}{\subhead{Evaluation Research}}
\item Created {\bf DistortBench} (first author; CVPR 2026 and ICML 2026 Workshops): 13.5K questions over 27 distortion types and 5 severity levels. Of 18 VLMs, the best (Qwen3.5-27B) scores {\bf 61.9\%} (chance 25\%), above the average annotator (60.2\%) and below a \mbox{3-annotator} majority vote (65.7\%).
\item Co-authored {\bf JudgeBreak} (ICML 2026 Workshop): across 95 subjective settings, linear probes on Qwen3-32B hidden states with \mbox{cross-validated} layer selection reach {\bf 78.1\%} mean pairwise accuracy vs.\ 70.1\% for the best reward model or \mbox{zero-shot} GPT-5.4 judge.
