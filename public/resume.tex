\begin{rSection}{Summary}
\raggedright
I am an Applied Scientist at Adobe, and my interests lie in multimodal language models. At Adobe, I train and evaluate them for creative work. The work often hinges on subjective quality, like whether a photo edit looks better or marketing copy feels original, so I spend a lot of my time building evaluations that can be used to reliably train and compare models. I have shipped this work in Adobe GenStudio and published it at CVPR, ICML, and AAAI workshops.
\end{rSection}

\begin{rSection}{Experience}

\begin{rSubsection}{Applied Scientist -- Adobe Inc. (Unified Platform), San Jose, CA}{June 2021 -- Present}{}{}

\item[] \vspace{0.1em}{\hspace{-1em}\textit{Vision-Language Model Training \& Evaluation}}
\item Led {\bf ATune} (under review): a 54M-parameter model that learns photo retouching from 10M unedited stock photographs and drives \mbox{off-the-shelf} editors through their sliders. In a study with 157 raters, its edits beat GPT-6 Astra on the same editor ({\bf 54.3\%} of 3,524 votes, 95\% CI 50.3--58.3) and tied with Claude Opus 5.
\item Fine-tuned {\bf Gemma-3-12B} with LoRA to score \mbox{marketing-image} quality, training on 8$\times$ H100 (DDP, BF16, FlashAttention-2, Liger kernels).
\item Grounded VLM quality reasoning in 14+ computed \mbox{pixel-level} metrics (sharpness, exposure, color balance); the resulting interpretable scorer reaches {\bf Pearson r $\approx$ 0.88} (MAE 0.77) vs.\ \mbox{zero-shot} GPT-5.4 (MAE 1.34) and Claude Opus 4.7 (MAE 1.95).
\item Built the {\bf MQCore} evaluation benchmark and a \mbox{fault-tolerant} async annotation pipeline (100+ images/min) for the 700K-image KADIS \mbox{image-quality} dataset.

\item[] \vspace{0.3em}{\hspace{-1em}\textit{Evaluation Research \& Benchmarking}}
\item First author of {\bf DistortBench} (CVPR 2026 Workshop): a 13.5K-question benchmark over 27 distortion types and 5 severity levels. Evaluated 18 VLMs with bootstrap confidence intervals; the best model (Qwen3.5-27B) scores {\bf 61.9\%} (chance 25\%), above the average annotator (60.2\%) and within the 95\% CI of a \mbox{3-annotator} majority vote (65.7\%, CI 59.4--71.5).
\item Co-authored {\bf JudgeBreak} (ICML 2026 Workshop): across 95 subjective evaluation settings, linear probes on Qwen3-32B hidden states with \mbox{cross-validated} layer selection reach {\bf 78.1\%} mean pairwise accuracy vs.\ 70.1\% for the strongest baseline (the best reward model or a \mbox{zero-shot} GPT-5.4 judge).
\item Co-authored a curiosity-driven {\bf LLM-as-a-judge} (AAAI 2026 Workshop, Oral) that personalizes \mbox{creative-writing} evaluation by modeling the judge's surprise at each expert's explanation, improving F1 by 23\% and correlation by 38\% over an SFT baseline.

\item[] \vspace{0.3em}{\hspace{-1em}\textit{Production Multimodal Systems \& Agents}}
\item Built the first prototype of {\bf Adobe GenStudio for Performance Marketing} (launched October 2024, now serving Fortune 500 clients including Microsoft and AT\&T), then designed its multimodal brand service (VLM content validation, \mbox{brand-DNA} extraction, retrieval), deployed in 3 regions.
\item Built a {\bf multimodal Photoshop agent} that edits images from \mbox{natural-language} instructions, running an \mbox{observe-think-act} loop over 49 tools with vision grounding and a \mbox{quality-critic} refinement step.
\end{rSubsection}
