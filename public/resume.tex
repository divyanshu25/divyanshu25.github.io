\item Trained {\bf \mbox{Qwen3.5-4B}} LoRA reward models on a {\bf 1.1M soft-labeled dataset} curated from 14 \mbox{human-rated} sources, raising KonIQ correlation to {\bf 0.663 SRCC} (vs.\ 0.421 for the reference model).
\item Curated a {\bf \mbox{67K-item} held-out benchmark}, \mbox{leak-checked} against all training images via SSCD copy detection, and gated {\bf 10 model releases} on a \mbox{pre-registered} \mbox{non-inferiority} test.
\item Conceived and built a {\bf live demo suite} that turns the judge from a scorer into a {\bf creative engine}: it \mbox{auto-crops} images ({\bf 0.82 pairwise accuracy on GAIC} vs.\ 0.71 for the \mbox{score-based} baseline), retouches photos, scores marketing and custom attributes, \mbox{re-ranks} Adobe Stock for marketing use, and flags \mbox{low-quality} assets. Presented it to {\bf leadership and external partners}, opening new customer engagements.
\item Caught a run whose vision encoder collapsed within 200 steps, leaving every checkpoint blind to the image, and added an automated {\bf \mbox{vision-health} check} to the training pipeline.

\item[] \vspace{0.3em}{\subhead{ATune: Unsupervised Photo Retouching (Co-Lead with Adobe Research)}}
\item Co-led project design with Adobe Research as \mbox{co-first} author, owning the synthetic data pipeline, training recipe, and human evaluation framework, and {\bf mentored an intern} on the project.
