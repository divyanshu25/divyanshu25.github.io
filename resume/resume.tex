\item Evaluated against frontier models in a \mbox{157-rater} human preference study (3,524 judgments), achieving {\bf 54.3\% of decisive votes against GPT-6 Astra} (95\% CI 50.3--58.3) and {\bf matching Claude Opus 5} (50.5\%).

\item Diagnosed \mbox{vision-encoder} collapse that made training checkpoints \mbox{image-blind} within 200 steps despite normal loss curves; implemented automated {\bf \mbox{vision-health} monitoring} to detect and halt silent training failures.
